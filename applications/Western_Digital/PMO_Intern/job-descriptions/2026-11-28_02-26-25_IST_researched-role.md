## Role context

The listing describes an **IT PMO Project Analyst Intern** in Bengaluru supporting IT project management and PMO processes.

## Main work described

- Create templates for IT projects.
- Partner with the PMO to create, track and report project information across functional teams.
- Provide technical support for PMO tools and processes.
- Document PMO processes and create end-user training materials.
- Provide training to end users of PMO solutions and processes.
- Track project deliverables and milestones against approved timelines and budget.
- Use Western Digital's System Development Lifecycle and project-management methodology across development, customization, configuration, testing and implementation of business and IT systems.
- Coordinate day-to-day operational and tactical aspects of approved projects.

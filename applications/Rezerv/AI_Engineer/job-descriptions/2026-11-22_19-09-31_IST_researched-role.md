# Researched AI Engineer role summary

Accessed 22 November 2026. This is a paraphrased research summary, separate from the user-supplied screenshot.

Source: [Adrian Ng's Rezerv AI Engineer post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ngarte_were-hiring-an-ai-engineer-remote-position-activity-7492366264531615745-Vj0D).

Rezerv seeks an engineer to build an agent layer for its studio SaaS: client-activated support, sales, marketing and operations agents; WhatsApp lead-to-booking workflows; website chat; a dashboard copilot; and an MCP server. It seeks at least two years in engineering, production LLM delivery, deployed agent experience, TypeScript/Node.js proficiency, and attention to response time, usage cost and evaluations. The location is remote from Myanmar with the Yangon team. The post mentions bonus and possible equity. Apply by emailing a CV and a short account of an agent system delivered to careers@rezerv.co.
